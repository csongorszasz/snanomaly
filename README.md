# snanomaly | Supernova Anomaly Detection

## Why care?
A _supernova_ is the dramatic explosion of certain types of stars at the end of their stellar evolution.<br><br>
The study of supernovae led to the discovery that our Universe is expanding at an accelerating pace (not decelerating and not constant). This won the [Nobel Prize in Physics 2011](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2011/press-release/).

## Objective
Create a pipeline that can detect anomalies in light curves of supernovae.<br><br>
Anomalies can be the source of simple data bugs or yet undiscovered astrophysical phenomena.
